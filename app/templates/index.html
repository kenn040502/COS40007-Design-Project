Project Summary

This system analyses Malaysian household income, expenditure, poverty, and inequality data from the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM). Two AI/ML techniques are applied: ARIMA time series forecasting on the Gini coefficient (1974–2030) and K-Means clustering to group the 160 administrative districts by socioeconomic profile.
