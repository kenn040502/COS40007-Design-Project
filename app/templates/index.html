Project Summary

This system analyses Malaysia's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation across 13 divisions and 16 states, applying ARIMA time series forecasting on the national inflation trend and K-Means clustering to group states by their CPI and income profile. Fuel price dynamics and their correlation with inflation are also examined. Data sourced from the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) open data portal.

Does Fuel Improve Inflation Prediction? (ARIMAX vs ARIMA)

Correlation shows association; this tests predictive value. Two models are fit on the fuel/CPI overlap window and evaluated on the same held-out test period: ARIMAX (fuel as an exogenous regressor) vs a univariate ARIMA. A lower RMSE for ARIMAX means fuel adds genuine forecasting signal. The headline national forecast stays univariate because future fuel prices are unknown.
